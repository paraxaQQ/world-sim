## question

When models receive the same survival rules, partial information, repeated chances to interact, and no instruction to cooperate or compete, what costly social behavior appears?

Session 1 ended with cooperative language but no costly transfer. Session 2 asks a narrower prospective question: when shelter construction requires resource consolidation, does any objectively costly transfer produce a shelter before the action deadline?

This treatment was chosen after observing session 1. It is exploratory. It cannot establish a memory effect, reputation effect, stable model trait, or population rate.

## outcomes

Primary outcome:

> by the end of chance 3, one or more completed `give_wood` transfers produce enough owned wood for the recipient to complete `build_shelter` in cycle 2.

The outcome requires objective `resource_given` and later `shelter_built` events. A valid gift pays the actor's 1-energy action cost. Shelter construction pays 2 energy and spends 4 wood. Gifts resolve before personal actions, so a simultaneous transfer and build can complete in one chance.

Record separately:

- any completed costly resource transfer
- every completed wood gift, giver, recipient, amount, and chance
- wood received by each survivor
- shelter-enabling wood and the later builder
- transfers without a later shelter
- attempted transfers that pay energy but move no resource
- reciprocal transfers
- shelters, final energy, deaths, messages, rests, and deadline cancellations

Speech is context only. Speech without a completed transfer scores zero. Keeping wood is not labeled defection. A completed transfer and shelter is a coordinated trace, not proof of a binding agreement or stable disposition.
