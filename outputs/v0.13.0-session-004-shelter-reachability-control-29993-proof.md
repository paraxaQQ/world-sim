# shelter reachability control

## claim

from the exact session-2 parent and the same shared-wood transition used for session 4, the engine permits a valid costly wood transfer to enable a shelter. the tape succeeds by beat 3 under every initiative phase.

## result

the parent artifact SHA-256 is `fc0b07dfc404a2f485f3b6a2c2f191fec5e495153d6147d428d6cb251cab27fe`. its canonical result is `ed1f299bbc698951e77256b46291ea4ee142469bc0a7cd0e7b6bf476820392ca`. exact parent replay passed before the control ran.

the control restored day 2, selected `sequential-dialogue-v3`, changed shared wood from `2` to `0`, and used this fixed tape:

- Cinder gives Lumen 2 wood and later rests.
- Lumen submits `build_shelter` and later rests.
- Aster and Birch rest.

the gift resolved at event sequence `158`. the shelter resolved afterward at sequence `161`, both in beat 1. all four survivors lived and Lumen ended with a shelter.

## bound

this proves physical reachability: at least one fixed, valid action tape succeeds under every initiative phase. it does not estimate whether a model will discover or choose that tape. it also does not make the generic calibration policy a behavioral stand-in for the live models.
