## diagnosis

The request used `reasoning_effort: low`, but current DeepSeek V4 documentation says `low` and `medium` are compatibility aliases for `high`. Thinking is enabled by default. The 1,024-token budget was therefore consumed before a final JSON choice was completed. See the [DeepSeek thinking-mode documentation](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/thinking_mode).

The next attempt needs a deliberate choice: disable long thinking for the compatibility smoke, or authorize a larger completion budget for a reasoning smoke. This attempt does not justify another paid call automatically.
