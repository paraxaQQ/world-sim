## bound

This proves the calibrated live path, strict response compatibility for this route, hard call limiting, byte-retained provenance, and exact replay for one run. It does not prove cooperation, communication, strategic planning, stable Nemotron behavior, deadline understanding, or behavior by DeepSeek, MiniMax, Kimi, or GLM. Repeating `forage`, resting once, and missing three deadlines are objective traces, not evidence of intent.
