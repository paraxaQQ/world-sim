## interaction protocol

Use `sequential-dialogue-v3` for one four-beat day. Every response contains one sealed physical action and optional speech.

- awake survivors answer in rotating initiative order
- valid speech becomes visible immediately to eligible later speakers in the same beat
- submitted actions and physical outcomes remain hidden until the complete beat is collected
- all submitted actions then resolve atomically under the v2 physical phase order
- `wait` costs zero and keeps the survivor awake
- `rest` ends that survivor's day
- on beat 4, valid speech still commits, but only `rest` can resolve physically
- an incomplete provider beat retains committed speech and submissions but applies no physical mutation from that beat

The day-3, beat-1 initiative is Cinder, Lumen, Aster, Birch. It rotates on later beats around the same permanent seat ring.

Record separately:

- every attempted and completed food or wood transfer
- every shelter construction

- reciprocal gifts, gifts without a later shelter, deaths, exhaustion, and final private state

Speech alone scores zero costly cooperation. A message claiming an action is unverified until the engine records the physical event. A transfer-and-build trace is coordination evidence in this episode, not proof of trust, morality, stable personality, or model-wide behavior.
