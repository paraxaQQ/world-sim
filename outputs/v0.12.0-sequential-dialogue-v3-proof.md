# v0.12.0 sequential-dialogue-v3 proof

## claim

The retained `sequential-dialogue-v3` calibration matches the retained `global-beats-v2` calibration on every recorded aggregate physical metric and per-seed physical comparison. A focused fixed-choice engine test also matches physical state and objective events across three seeds and all four seat rotations.

Within a v3 beat, survivors speak in rotating initiative order. Valid speech is committed immediately, so later speakers can reply during the same beat. Physical choices remain sealed until every survivor has submitted, then resolve atomically under the existing v2 phase order.

## failure and integrity receipts

The targeted tests exercise the intended trust path. An earlier survivor can promise and submit a gift. A later survivor can hear the promise and submit a build choice. The sealed transfer can fund that build only during atomic resolution.

They also prove the failure barrier. If a provider fails before all submissions arrive, already committed dialogue remains visible, but no physical action, energy cost, rest transition, or exhaustion is applied from the incomplete beat.

Format-v6 verifier tests reconstruct completed requests, views, raw replies, parsed choices, cost authorizations, and results. They require the provider-reported model to match the assigned model. They also replay failed partial beats and reject tampering with the committed prefix, numeric JSON types, or no-call budget and call-cap failures.

## claim boundary

No live model behavior is measured by this receipt, and no live session 4 is included or claimed. The retained calibration artifacts record aggregate metrics and the paired per-seed survival comparison; they do not retain exhaustive choice tapes. The focused engine test holds submitted choices fixed across three seeds and four seat rotations. Together, those receipts establish mechanics, the stated physical-outcome parity, replay, and the failure boundary for this calibration design.

Same-beat dialogue is supposed to let later survivors change their submissions after hearing earlier speech. A live model run can therefore diverge from v2, and this receipt does not predict whether models will cooperate, defect, deceive, trust promises, or survive more often.
