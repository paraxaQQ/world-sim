# v0.10.0 global-beats-v2 proof

## claim

Fresh worlds use one shared within-day event loop. Every awake survivor chooses one action and optional speech from a frozen view of the same beat. The engine resolves the beat only after it has every proposal.

`wait` costs zero energy and keeps the survivor awake. `rest` ends the survivor's day. Speech sent on one beat becomes available on the recipient's next active beat. Transfers are atomic against holdings at the start of the transfer phase, and successful transfers can fund eating or shelter construction later in that same beat.

Legacy `slots-v1` artifacts keep their original semantics and exact replay bytes.

It also covers transfer-funded same-beat construction, relay rejection, reciprocal gifts, exact replay, legacy reconstruction, protocol-tamper rejection, and failure before partial beat resolution.
