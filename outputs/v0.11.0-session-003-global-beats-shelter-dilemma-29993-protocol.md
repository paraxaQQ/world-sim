## interaction protocol

Session 3 uses `global-beats-v2`:

- one day contains four shared beats
- every awake survivor chooses from a frozen view of the same unresolved beat
- one response contains one action and optional speech
- `wait` costs zero and leaves the survivor awake
- `rest` ends the survivor's day
- speech sent on one beat arrives on the recipient's next active beat
- transfers are checked against transfer-phase-start holdings
- completed transfers can fund eating or shelter construction later in the same beat
- only `rest` resolves on beat 4; another choice and its speech are cancelled before exhaustion
